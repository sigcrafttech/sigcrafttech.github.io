CALONE / macOS

CaleOne for Mac

A compact calendar and clock in your Mac menu bar.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Get started

  1. Open CaleOne and look for its date and time in the menu bar. CaleOne does not appear in the Dock.
  2. Click the menu bar clock to browse months. Use the middle navigation control to return to today.
  3. Choose Preferences to follow the system time format, select 12-hour or 24-hour time, or enter a custom format.
  4. In Preferences, choose English or Follow System. Supported system languages include English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
  5. Enable Launch CaleOne at login only if you want the app to open automatically. macOS may ask you to approve the login item.

Local, focused, and private

CaleOne works offline, without an account, ads, or tracking. It displays dates and time; it does not create calendar events, manage reminders, or sync calendar accounts.
