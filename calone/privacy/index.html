CalOne Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

CalOne is provided by SignalCraft Technology Limited. This policy describes the data practices of the CalOne macOS app.

Data collected by the app

CalOne does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. It has no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. You do not need to create an account.

Local operation and settings

The app reads the date, time, time zone, and language/time-format preferences provided by macOS to display its clock and calendar. App preferences, such as the chosen language mode, time format, custom format, and icon visibility, are saved locally using macOS preferences. CalOne does not upload these settings or sync them to a company server.

Calendar data and permissions

CalOne displays a date calendar. It does not access your calendar events, reminders, contacts, microphone, camera, screen contents, or location. Launch at login is optional and is enabled only when you choose it in Preferences, using the macOS login-item service.

Network access and external links

CalOne's clock, calendar, and preferences work offline and do not make network requests. Clicking the date-format help button opens https://www.w3.org/TR/NOTE-datetime in your default browser. That browser and website handle the resulting connection under their own privacy practices. Opening macOS Date & Time settings launches the relevant system settings page.

App Store and Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads, purchases, and updates. Apple may process purchase, download, and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings. This policy describes CalOne's own practices and does not replace Apple's privacy policy.

Support

If you email servers@sigcrafttech.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so that we can respond to your request. This support correspondence is separate from the app, which does not send us data automatically. Contact the same address with privacy questions or requests about your support correspondence.

Changes

If CalOne's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
